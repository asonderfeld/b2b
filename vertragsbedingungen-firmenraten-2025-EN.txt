1. Subject Matter

This agreement governs the accommodation of employees and business partners at local corporate special rates. Schlossbräu mk | hotels GmbH acts solely as a service provider for arranging and providing corporate rates for the listed hotels. The agreement takes effect upon signature by both parties, between the hotels listed in the rate schedule and the company, as well as its subsidiary and sister companies.

The parties acknowledge that the individual contracts for each booking are concluded exclusively between the booking guest and the hotel operator.


2. Contract Term

This agreement becomes valid upon signature by both parties and automatically ends on 31 December 2025, without requiring termination. This agreement only becomes valid if an authorized representative of the company signs it digitally via the link contained in the email within 30 days of delivery. Should this not occur within 30 days, the offer lapses.


3. General

The stated prices apply per room/night and are non-commissionable unless otherwise noted.

All prices include taxes unless stated otherwise. Should the statutory VAT rate change, we reserve the right to adjust the agreed prices accordingly.

Prices and room categories are subject to availability at the respective hotels.

The prices agreed here apply to employees and guests of the signing company as well as all affiliated companies. In the interest of both parties, the company shall send the hotel a list of all affiliated companies, including address and contact person, as an appendix to the signed agreement.

The agreed corporate rates apply to all bookings of fewer than ten rooms. For group bookings of ten or more rooms, separate room rates and conditions apply, to be negotiated on a case-by-case basis.

Please note that the agreed corporate rates are valid on weekdays. On weekends and public holidays, they apply upon request and subject to availability.

The general terms and conditions of the listed hotels apply; we are happy to provide these upon request.


4. Communication within the Company

The company undertakes to communicate the special corporate rate terms internally, so that as many booking parties as possible make use of the agreement.

The company undertakes to fully inform its employees, as well as any third parties who are to benefit from this agreement, about the agreed terms and conditions. Any disadvantages resulting from a failure to provide this information shall be borne solely by the company.


5. Cancellation Terms

Unless otherwise stated on the reservation confirmation, free cancellation is possible until 6:00 p.m. on the day of arrival. In the event of cancellation after this deadline or in the case of a no-show, 100% of the first night's stay will be charged. During trade fair periods or for group bookings, separate cancellation deadlines apply, as stated in the respective booking confirmation.


6. Booking Guarantee

Every direct booking is considered a guaranteed reservation and will be held even after 6:00 p.m. on the day of arrival.


7. Payment

Payment is generally due upon arrival, unless a cost coverage agreement is in place. Cost coverage is only accepted via advance payment (payment received prior to the guest's arrival).

We are happy to send you a payment link for direct payment by credit card or instant bank transfer for each reservation.


8. Booking Conditions

Bookings under this agreement may only be made via the hotel contact details listed in the appendix.

The special rate terms apply only to direct bookings made with explicit reference to this agreement.

Price changes cannot be made after a booking has been confirmed.


9. Rate Terms

From the date this agreement takes effect, mk | hotels grants the company the special rate terms listed on the final page for rooms in the standard category of the respective hotel. mk | hotels is also happy to offer the company additional room categories upon request and subject to availability, for an additional charge.

The special terms agreed in this contract are valid outside of trade fair periods only.

For group bookings of ten or more rooms, separate room rates and conditions apply, to be negotiated on a case-by-case basis.
